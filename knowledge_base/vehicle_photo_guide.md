# Vehicle Photo Evidence Guide

The primary photo is reviewed only after a claims officer confirms that the submitted documents may proceed. Additional photos remain available as supporting evidence.

## File and resolution requirements

The primary photo must be a JPEG or PNG file no larger than 10 MB. The minimum accepted resolution is 1024 by 768 pixels.

A photo below the minimum resolution fails the technical evidence gate even when it appears sharp and well lit. Upload the original full-resolution photo rather than a thumbnail, messaging-app preview, or compressed screenshot.

## What the photo should show

The image should:

- Show a real physical vehicle.
- Show the damaged area clearly.
- Include enough of the vehicle to understand the damage location.
- Be in focus and adequately lit.
- Avoid glare, obstruction, extreme cropping, and excessive distance.
- Avoid illustrations, rendered images, screenshots, stock images, and visible watermarks.

Vehicle visibility must reach 70% and damage visibility must reach 60% for the current evidence-suitability checks.

## Technical image quality

Technical quality combines resolution, sharpness, brightness, and contrast. A score of 80% or higher is classified as good, while 60% to 79.99% is marginal.

Technical quality and eligibility are different. A photo may receive a good technical-quality score but still be ineligible when a mandatory requirement, such as minimum resolution, supported format, visible vehicle, visible damage, or absence of a watermark, is not met.

## Raw confidence and eligibility-adjusted score

Raw confidence summarizes the reliability of observations such as vehicle visibility, damage visibility, damaged parts, damage location, and severity. It is not the probability that a claim should be approved.

The eligibility-adjusted score applies mandatory evidence rules after raw confidence is calculated. If any mandatory rule fails, the adjusted score becomes 0%, even when raw confidence is high. The failed rule is shown in the assessment findings so claims staff can request a suitable replacement photo.

## Image integrity review

The portal checks whether the image appears to be a real photograph, contains a stock-image watermark, or presents an uncertain image-origin signal. An uncertain signal routes the image for human verification; it does not prove that the image was generated or manipulated.

Illustrations, renders, images without a real visible vehicle, and watermarked stock images are not eligible vehicle evidence.

## Damage severity

- Minor: limited cosmetic damage such as small scratches or dents.
- Moderate: damage to parts such as a bumper, grille, headlight, or body panel that requires professional inspection.
- Severe: extensive crushing, structural damage, fire or flood damage, or damage across multiple major areas.

Moderate or severe visible damage is routed to a claims specialist. This is a review route, not an automatic rejection.
