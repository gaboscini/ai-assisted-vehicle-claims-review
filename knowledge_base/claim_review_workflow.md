# Claim Review Workflow

The portal separates document review from vehicle-photo review so claims staff can stop incomplete or inconsistent submissions before running the more time-consuming photo assessment.

## Stage 1: Customer submission

The customer provides claim details, KTP, SIM, STNK, one required primary vehicle-damage photo, and any optional supporting photos. The portal saves the submission and issues a claim ID.

## Stage 2: Document review

The required documents are checked against the customer, policy, vehicle, and incident records. Claims staff see the extracted results, confidence thresholds, match scores, findings, and recommended route.

The claims officer may continue to vehicle-photo assessment, request corrected evidence, mark the claim for investigation, or reject the claim with a recorded reason.

## Stage 3: Vehicle-photo assessment

Vehicle-photo assessment begins only when the claims officer allows the documents to proceed. Only the required primary photo is assessed automatically. The review covers technical quality, vehicle and damage visibility, image integrity, damaged parts, damage location, visible severity, and routing conditions.

If document review results in rejection, investigation, or a request for more information, vehicle-photo assessment does not start.

## Stage 4: Final decision

When document and vehicle-photo results are ready, the claims officer reviews the complete submission and records one action:

- Approve the claim to proceed.
- Reject the claim.
- Request more information.
- Mark the claim for investigation.

The recorded customer message appears in Track a Claim. Internal confidence scores, detailed assessment findings, and claims-officer notes are not shown on the customer page.

## Review recommendations

- Continue standard review when required evidence checks pass.
- Request replacement evidence when a document or photo is missing, unreadable, expired, inconsistent, or technically ineligible.
- Send the claim for specialist review when damage is moderate or severe or when image integrity or consistency requires verification.
- Final coverage and claim decisions always remain with authorized claims staff.
