# Vehicle Claim Submission Guide

This guide describes the information and evidence accepted by the current vehicle claims portal. A complete submission requires KTP, SIM, STNK, and one primary vehicle-damage photo, together with the policyholder and incident information described below. Coverage and final claim decisions remain subject to the applicable insurance policy.

## Information to prepare

Before starting a claim, prepare the policy number, policyholder name, mobile number, incident date, incident location, and a clear description of what happened and what was damaged.

The policy number and policyholder name must match the policy record. The policy must also have been active on the incident date before evidence can be submitted.

## Required documents

Every submission requires clear images of these documents:

- KTP identity document
- SIM driver licence
- STNK vehicle registration document

Use the original document image. The complete document should be visible, readable, correctly oriented, and free from glare or obstruction. The portal currently accepts PNG, JPG, and JPEG uploads up to 10 MB per file.

## Required vehicle photo

One primary vehicle-damage photo is required. It must show the real physical vehicle and the damaged area clearly. Up to two additional photos may be included as supporting evidence, but the primary photo is the image used for the initial vehicle-photo assessment.

## Submission and claim tracking

After submission, the customer receives a claim ID. Keep this claim ID and use Track a Claim to view the latest status.

Common status meanings are:

- Submitted: the claim was received and initial review is in progress.
- Ready for review: results are available to claims staff.
- Needs information: corrected or replacement evidence is required.
- Investigation: the claim requires additional verification.
- Approved or rejected: an authorized claims officer recorded the decision.

## Replacement evidence

When a claim needs more information, the tracking page lists the exact documents or photos that must be replaced. Upload every requested item and submit it again. The claim then returns to review.

Do not create a new claim when the existing claim is waiting for replacement evidence.

## Decision responsibility

Automated checks support evidence review but do not approve or reject an insurance claim. An authorized claims officer reviews the findings, verifies coverage, and records the final decision.
